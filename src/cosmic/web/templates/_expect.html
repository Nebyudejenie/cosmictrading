How this was tested
  • The auto-trader's own rule, {{ e.rule }}, on completed {{ e.bar_word }} bars: it buys at the next bar's opening price (at the ask) after the rule turns long, and sells (at the bid) after it turns flat.
  • Every trade has its stop 3×ATR below the entry. A price that jumps past the stop sells at the worse price.
  • Every trade is sized to your per-trade limit in whole lots. A trade too big for it is skipped, never made bigger. Your daily stop, trades a day, open trades, money and total loss limit apply as they do live, and so does the half size after a deep drop.
  • Costs: {% for c in e.costs %}{{ c.market }}: fee {{ c.fee_pct|dec(3) }} % a side (ASSUMPTION from your risk policy), spread {{ c.spread_pct|dec(3) }} % ({% if c.spread_measured %}measured on the venue's own recent prices{% else %}ASSUMPTION: no venue prices stored{% endif %}), {{ swap_text(c.swap) }}; {% endfor %}slippage {{ e.slippage_pct|dec(2) }} % a fill (ASSUMPTION).
  • Not included: delays and requotes, and the gap between these prices and the venue's own.
