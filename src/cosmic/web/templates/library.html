{{ total }} published rules, tested once with their published settings on BTC and gold. Historical simulations with assumed costs (ASSUMPTION): not evidence of future profit. Testing many rules makes the best look good partly by luck; the deflated Sharpe probability corrects for that.

How to read this

  • Timing Sharpe: the rule's returns minus holding its own average position all the time. Positive means its timing added something; a rule that is simply invested in a rising market scores zero.
  • Deflated probability: how likely the timing skill is real after allowing for all {{ view.run.trials if view.run else "the" }} trials. Qualifying needs 0.95.
  • {{ tier("QUALIFIED") }} significant, held up in the validation years, profitable at double costs, ≥ 10 trades, drawdown no worse than buy-and-hold. {{ tier("WATCH") }} timing positive in both periods but not proven. {{ tier("REJECTED") }} everything else.
  • Only QUALIFIED or WATCH rules can be switched on, for paper proposals only; every trade still needs your approval.
