How the competition works

  1. Same trading as live: each rule buys at the next price after its signal, sells on its exit or at a stop 3×ATR below, sized to the per-trade limit, with the daily limits and the costs below. Results are in R, the most one trade may lose.
  2. Chosen on the earlier part: after a warm-up of {{ protocol.warm_up }} bars, the first {{ protocol.dev_pct }} % of each series. A rule goes on to the check only with at least {{ protocol.min_trades["1h"] }} trades (hourly) or {{ protocol.min_trades["1d"] }} (daily), a positive result, and a t-statistic of at least {{ protocol.dev_t }}.
  3. Checked on the later part it never saw: it must make money there too, at double costs as well, with a t-statistic of {{ "%.2f"|format(run.result.val_threshold) }}. That bar rises with the number of rules checked, so that trying many rules cannot make one look good by luck.
  4. Proven forward: since {{ protocol.forward_from.strftime("%d %b %Y") }}, a date fixed before any of these prices existed, every rule is scored on new prices only. A qualified rule earns the auto-trader after {{ protocol.forward_trades["1h"] }} forward trades (hourly) or {{ protocol.forward_trades["1d"] }} (daily) in profit.
  5. You decide: an earned rule is reviewed and added in a code change, and the auto-trader uses it only when you start it with that rule. Nothing here trades by itself.
