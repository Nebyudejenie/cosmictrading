Add an MT5 account

  1. Details (this page): nickname, login, server, password, type.
  2. Connection test: the bridge signs MT5 in and reads the account from the broker.
  3. Verification: the broker's own answer: which broker, DEMO or REAL, server, currency, balance.
  4. Save: only a verified account can be saved and used.
Broker (MT5 confirms it in the test; a wrong choice is refused)

The list shows, by broker, the servers the brokers themselves named to our terminals; choose one of your own broker. MetaTrader 5 cannot list a broker's servers, so your Exness server appears here only once terminal 3 is signed in to your Exness account on the bridge computer's screen; until then type it exactly as Exness's Personal Area shows it. A server of the other broker is refused at once.

Account type (the broker confirms it in the test; a wrong choice is refused)

Encrypted the moment you save; never shown again, logged or sent anywhere but to MT5 on the bridge computer when it signs in.

You do not choose a terminal: the system puts the account on the MetaTrader 5 that is already signed in to it, or on a free one.
