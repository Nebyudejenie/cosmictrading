How it works

  1. It watches its markets on {{ b.bars }}{% if b.timeframe == "1d" %} (it decides once a day, usually within the hour after 00:00 UTC){% endif %}, with the {{ b.rule }} rule.
  2. When {% if is_breakout %}a price breaks out above its recent high{% else %}the rule turns long{% endif %}, it {% if m and m.mode == "ASK" %}asks you on Telegram (TIME TO TRADE){% else %}buys{% endif %}, sized so a loss at the stop stays within your limit, and places the stop with the order.
  3. It sells when {% if is_breakout %}the price falls below its recent low{% else %}the rule turns flat{% endif %}, or the stop sells first.
  4. It stops by itself after the daily loss limit, at the trade and position limits, and whenever the emergency stop is on. Every trade still passes every risk check.
  5. It trades smaller after a bad run: five losing trades' worth below its best result, it sizes every trade to half your limit, until the drop is back under two and a half.
  6. It switches itself off for good when its losses reach your total loss limit (ten losing trades' worth): it starts a trade only if a full loss on it and on the open ones stays within that limit.
