How it works

  1. It watches gold and bitcoin every hour, with the {{ b.rule }} rule.
  2. When a price breaks out above its recent high, it {% if m and m.mode == "ASK" %}asks you on Telegram (TIME TO TRADE){% else %}buys{% endif %}, sized so a loss at the stop stays within your limit, and places the stop with the order.
  3. It sells when the price falls below its recent low, or the stop sells first.
  4. It stops by itself after the daily loss limit, at the trade and position limits, and whenever the emergency stop is on. Every trade still passes every risk check.

Demo money ({{ label("SIMULATED") }}). The rule was tested on daily bars; on hourly bars this is a forward test. Past results do not promise future results.
