Trend: the 8- and 21-candle averages and where the price is. Support and resistance: the low and high of the last 20 candles. HFM's candles are built from its price every second since 4 Nov 2026, and from its prices stored every ~15 seconds before (whose highs and lows may miss a quick move); with bridge version 4, MT5's own candles replace them.

The rules

  • BUY: the hourly trend is up, the daily trend is not down, and the 1-minute momentum (RSI) turns up through 50 above its average.
  • SELL: the mirror: hourly down, daily not up, the 1-minute momentum turning down below its average.
  • WAIT: anything else; also when the hourly volatility is too quiet or too wild, or the candles are too few or too old.
  • A decision is not a trade: your simulation or HFM demo sizes it within your limits, or waits and says why.
